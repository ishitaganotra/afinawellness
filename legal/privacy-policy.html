Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Afina ("we" or "our") helps you track your hormonal health, nutrition, and movement across every life stage. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and process your personal data when you use the Afina app (the "App") or other Afina products and services (the "Services") and the choices you have.

What Data We Collect

  • Email address and account credentials — collected when you create an account
  • Cycle information, symptoms, weight, and nutrition logs — collected when you enter or log them in the app
  • Exercise and fitness activity — collected when you log workouts, and from Apple Health or Oura if you choose to connect them
  • Messages, photos, and voice recordings you send to the AI Coach — collected when you use the AI Coach, and only after you grant permission in the app
  • Anonymous usage analytics and crash reports — collected automatically as you use the app

How Your Data Is Stored

  • Account, nutrition, and activity data: Your account, nutrition logs, exercise activity, and step counts are stored in our secure cloud backend (Supabase). Supabase servers are located in the United States.
  • Cycle and period data: Your menstrual cycle data — including period dates, cycle length, and symptoms — is stored locally on your device only and is never automatically synced to our servers.
  • Partner SMS exception: If you enable the Partner Updates feature, your most recent period date is sent to our backend solely to detect cycle changes and send your partner's weekly notification. This data is used for no other purpose.
  • AI Coach conversations: Messages you send to the AI Coach — along with photos you share and app context used to personalize replies (your cycle phase, symptoms, goals, food log, and any health data you've connected from Apple Health or Oura) — are transmitted to Anthropic's API to generate responses. Anthropic retains API inputs according to their standard data retention policy. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train their models. We do not store your conversation content on our servers. For details on Anthropic's data retention, see Anthropic's privacy center. You are asked for permission in the app before any of this data is shared.
  • Voice notes: If you use voice input in the AI Coach, your audio recording is transmitted to OpenAI's API solely to be transcribed into text. OpenAI does not use API inputs to train their models. See OpenAI's privacy policy for details on their data handling.
  • Subscription and billing: Subscription status and purchase records are managed through Apple In-App Purchases and subject to Apple's privacy policy.

When We Share Your Data

We share data with third-party subprocessors listed in the "Third-Party Services and Subprocessors" section above for the specific purposes described. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

We share personal data only with third parties that provide the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy. Each subprocessor is bound by contractual terms requiring them to process your data solely to provide their service to us, to keep it confidential and secure, and not to use it for their own purposes — including, for the AI services that power the AI Coach, a commitment not to use your data to train their models.

We may also disclose personal data when required by law, including in response to court orders or subpoenas. We will comply with applicable legal requirements — but we will attempt to notify you before doing so to the extent we are legally permitted, and will seek to narrow the scope of any such request where possible.

Reproductive and Menstrual Health Data

We treat your menstrual and reproductive health data as especially sensitive. We do not voluntarily share this data with law enforcement or any third party outside the scenarios described above.

Third-Party Services and Subprocessors

  • Anthropic — powers the AI Coach; messages are processed per Anthropic's API privacy terms
  • OpenAI — transcribes voice notes into text; audio is processed per OpenAI's privacy policy
  • Apple — handles subscription billing and management
  • Supabase — backend database for account and health data (US-based servers)
  • PostHog — anonymous usage analytics
  • Sentry — anonymous crash reporting

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your data. We do not share personal health information with advertisers. We do not use your health data to build advertising profiles.

Data Retention

We retain your account and health data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Data Breach Notification

If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you within the timeframe required by applicable law, and will describe the nature of the breach, the data affected, and the steps we are taking in response.

Your Rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about you, request deletion, correct inaccurate data, and receive a copy of your data. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law.

To exercise any of these rights: support@hormoniousapp.com

Age Requirement

You must be 18 or older to use this app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have collected data from someone under 18, please contact us immediately.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all personal data linked to your identity at any time by contacting support@hormoniousapp.com. We will process your deletion request and remove all identifiable data within 30 days. We may retain de-identified, aggregated, or anonymized data (such as usage patterns and retention metrics) for product analytics and improvement purposes, but this data cannot be linked back to you.

Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via the App or other means of notification as appropriate.
